# NO GUSTÓ · La misma foto con más calidad (Plaza de Axpe y Gamba roja, primera versión)

**Por qué no gustó:** salió exactamente el mismo ángulo que la foto original. Mejoró la calidad, pero cero creatividad: el dueño no quiere su foto mejorada, quiere una foto nueva y más profesional.

**Prompt que falló (gamba):**
@image1 is a real photograph of a red prawn from Palamós grilled and served whole at a Basque wood-fire grill restaurant. Recreate the same dish with every element of @image1 and nothing added: one whole deep red prawn with its head, long thin antennae and legs, the shell lightly marked by the grill and glistening, lying on the same square pale frosted glass plate on a white tablecloth. Keep the prawn's pose, size and anatomy exactly as in @image1. Change only light and camera: low-key still life, one narrow soft light from the upper left, the background and tablecloth edges falling into near black, the red of the shell the only strong colour. 90 mm lens at f/4, ISO 640, camera 45 cm away at 40 degrees above the table, vertical frame, prawn slightly below centre with dark space above it. Antennae crisp, a few tiny drops of oil on the glass. Photographic realism, no text.

**Prompt corregido (gamba):** usa la foto «only as the list of elements, not as a layout to copy» y cambia cámara, objetivo, encuadre y luz:
@image1 is a real photo of red prawns from Palamós grilled whole at a Basque wood-fire grill restaurant. Use it only as the list of elements, not as a layout to copy. The new photograph must contain the same real elements: two whole deep red prawns lying one over the other with heads, long antennae and legs, shells glistening with coarse salt and light grill marks, the square pale frosted glass plate, the white tablecloth. Exactly two prawns, correct anatomy. Nothing else added. New composition by a professional food photographer: a near table-level side view at 10 degrees, 100 mm macro at f/4, the two prawn heads large in the lower left, the antennae drawn as fine red lines arching across the dark upper part of the frame, the edge of the frosted glass plate glowing as a thin line of light. Low-key, one narrow back-side light from the upper right, everything else near black, red the only strong colour. Photographic realism, no text.
